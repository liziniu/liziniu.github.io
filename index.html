News

July 2026. Multiple works on long-horizon LLM reinforcement learning and post-training were accepted to ICML 2026.

April 2026. A discussion on X connected Claude Mythos to looped language models, an idea explored in our paper Scaling Latent Reasoning via Looped Language Models.

February 2026. I joined Tencent Hunyuan as a Research Scientist in the Qingyun Talent Program.

May 2025. I joined ByteDance Seed-Edge as an intern, starting frontier research on long-term AI directions.

January 2025. Our work on diversity-preserving supervised fine-tuning was accepted to ICLR 2025 and received Best Paper Runner-up at the NeurIPS 2024 FITML Workshop.

July 2024. ReMax appeared at ICML 2024, introducing a simple and efficient REINFORCE-style method for aligning large language models.
